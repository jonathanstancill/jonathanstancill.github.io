About me

I'm an aerospace engineering student at the Georgia Institute of Technology with a strong interest in aerodynamics, propulsion, and aircraft design.

My interest in engineering began with robotics and expanded through research involving hydrodynamics and propulsion. At Georgia Tech, working with GT Supersonics has given me opportunities to explore the engineering challenges behind high-speed flight.

I'm particularly interested in work that combines rigorous analysis with practical design, building, and testing.
